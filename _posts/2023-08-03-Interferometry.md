John Makous's LightWork Memo 31 covers both the theory and experimental results of additive interferometry.

+ [Basic theory of additive interferometry](https://wvurail.org/dspira-lessons/FilesUploaded/Interferometry_theory.pdf#page=1) - The theory section of LightWork Memo 31 explains how signals from two horns combine to produce interference fringes.

+ [Additive interferometry: experimental setup and results](https://wvurail.org/dspira-lessons/FilesUploaded/Interferometry_Additive_experimental.pdf#page=4) - The experimental section of the same memo describes a two-horn observation of the sun and how to interpret the results.

+ [Setting up a 2-horn interferometer](https://wvurail.org/dspira-lessons/FilesUploaded/SettingUp_2%20Horn_Interferometer.pdf) - Equipment, wiring, alignment, and GNU Radio instructions for adding and multiplying interferometers.
